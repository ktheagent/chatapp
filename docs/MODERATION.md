# Moderation and Appeals

## Principle

Reports are signals, not automatic proof.

## Enforcement record

Every enforcement action must include:
- case ID;
- reason category;
- policy reference;
- scope;
- start;
- expiry, when temporary;
- evidence references;
- whether automated detection contributed;
- appeal eligibility.

## Appeal states

- Submitted
- Assigned
- Reviewing
- More Information Required
- Decision Issued
- Restored

## Fairness guardrails

Do not use nationality, country code, ethnicity, or geography as a negative trust factor by themselves.

Monitor moderation outcomes by region for unexpected disparate impact.

## Mass-report defense

A coordinated burst of reports must not automatically create a permanent ban. Weight independent signals, historical recipient acceptance, report context where legally and technically available, account age, and automation patterns.
